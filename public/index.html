How it works, and one-time setup

Every day at noon Eastern a scan checks the Google Ads Transparency Center for each client's website, lists every advertiser running ads that send people there, and posts to that client's Slack channel. Anyone who isn't the client's official advertiser is flagged. New ones get screenshots and their ad copy in the thread.

Setting up a client

  1. Invite the Slack bot into the channel the reports should go to (/invite @YourBot).
  2. Click + Add client. Enter the website and the official advertiser name as Google shows it, and pick that channel from the list.
  3. Press Send test report. A report marked "Test report", with screenshots in the thread, appears in the channel a few minutes later. It does not count, so the first real report still marks everyone as new.
  4. Press Test scan. It posts nothing. In a few minutes this page lists every advertiser found.
  5. Find the client's own account in the list and press This is the client. That pins its ID, so a look-alike name can't pass as official.
  6. Done. The daily scan picks the client up automatically. Scan & post now sends a report immediately.

The first real report lists every non-official advertiser as new, because the tool hasn't seen any of them before. After that, only genuinely new advertisers and new ads get screenshots.
